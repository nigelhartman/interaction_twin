01

Show the pattern

Use this screen and your phone. A printer is optional.

The default pattern has 9 × 6 inner corners. Screen scaling is fine for camera intrinsics. Leave physical square size unknown unless you measure it.

02

Film varied views

A clear 15–30 second recording is a useful start.

  1. Keep the whole board visible. Move it around the image and tilt it in both directions. Keep reflections and motion blur low.
  2. Keep the camera settings consistent. Use the same lens, resolution, orientation, zoom, focus and image processing as your object capture.
  3. Then record each object clearly. Show different sides and moving parts without covering them. Capture alone does not make hidden geometry observable.
The worker samples at most 50 frames from the first 120 seconds. Every fourth sampled view is reserved before corner detection. One frontal view repeated many times is insufficient.
