What ran, and how to run it yourself

A genuine physics run

The unchanged generated appearance meshes accompany separate convex collision hulls. Newton integrates rigid-body motion under gravity and computes tabletop contacts. The browser displays the saved transforms and contact locations; it does not invent a placement animation.
