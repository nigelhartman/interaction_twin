About the footage
  • Actual owner video, saved SAM 3.1 masks and SAM 3D components. Connected replay uses estimated joints and documented geometry refinements; original assets remain available.
  • The phone inspection is a partial source-visible surface with estimated depth. It retains observed RGB and mask support, without completing hidden geometry.
  • The robot completes an authored kinematic preview: place and open the case, release both earbuds onto their targets, then close it. The right earbud uses a display copy of the better reconstructed left earbud; the original right-earbud model is preserved. Resting poses, grasp anchors and motion are authored presentation choices; grasp forces and physical contact are unverified.
  • The charging preview follows an instruction using phone and charger parts from different recordings. The cable, port targets and insertion paths are authored proxies; physical insertion, electrical fit and charging are not verified. The extended film also explains the saved source-linked MCP task draft.
