# Interaction Twin three minute demonstration

Updated 4 November 2026. Use genuine saved outputs and identify them as saved. Opening a page does not rerun the models. The new 45-second flagship remains separate from the full submission walkthrough.

## Core story

A demonstration contains more than shape. Interaction Twin keeps the parts, how they connect, the demonstrated steps and the evidence together, so a robotics developer or agent can reuse the interaction.

## Attribution and claim boundaries

The models and SDKs are upstream work. The application, representation, review system, agent tools and task authoring are the custom contribution. Identify event-built and pre-existing portions in the final submission.

Distinguish generated geometry, model proposals, source-supported motion, authored staging and the separate bounded Newton experiment. Do not claim successful charging, verified insertion, a trained robot, validated physical assembly or superiority over existing reconstruction research.
