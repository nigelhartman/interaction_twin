# Cobalt Motion v2 · Twin frame

**A solid physical part linked to its open digital twin.** The lower form represents the observed object. The open frame represents its reusable digital description. One clear connection joins them.

The mark uses the established cobalt `#3766ed`, white, ink `#151923` and paper `#f7f8fb`. Its two solid forms and open aperture remain readable at 24px. Keep the icon upright, preserve its proportions and allow space around it. Use a quiet fade/scale for video introductions, following the existing Cobalt Motion art direction.

The selected artwork is original vector geometry made for Interaction Twin. Product name and slogan remain **Interaction Twin** and **“Show it once. Keep how it works.”**
