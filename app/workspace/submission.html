02 / THE ONE-SENTENCE PITCH

Physical know-how
needs more than a mesh.

For teams turning recordings into
technical guides and simulation drafts.

Interaction Twin turns a recorded object interaction into separate 3D drafts and a source-backed procedure that agents can inspect through MCP.

01

The difficult task

A useful asset needs component boundaries, states, relationships and action order. Those usually live outside the mesh, in a person’s explanation.

02

The working approach

Meta perception and reasoning connect to local point tracking, geometry and a structured evidence program. Every stage can be inspected or replaced.

03

The honest boundary

The result is an authoring draft. Scale, complete shape, shared assembly, joints and contact still need validation before physics execution.
